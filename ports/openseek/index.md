- The shareware data is included: missions 1 and 2.
- The full version needs the original game data of the full Seek and Destroy, which is not included.
- To play it, convert your copy on a PC with openSEEK's `tools/build_pack.py` and copy the `assets` folder into `.local/share/love/openseek`.
- Saves and settings go to `.local/share/love/openseek`.
